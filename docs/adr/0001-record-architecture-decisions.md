# 0001 – Record Architecture Decisions

Date: 2026-02-06  
Status: Accepted

---

## Context

As the project grows, architectural decisions need to be documented
to ensure consistency and shared understanding among contributors.

---

## Decision

We will use **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)** stored in
`docs/adr/` to capture significant architectural decisions.

---

## Consequences

- Architectural intent is preserved over time
- New contributors can understand *why* decisions were made
- Slight overhead when introducing major changes
